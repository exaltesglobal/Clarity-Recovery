# Clarity Recovery: User Guide

Welcome. This guide shows how to set up Clarity Recovery so it protects you as well as it can, and how to use each feature. It is written for everyone, including people who don't usually change phone settings.

> **The short version:** open **Settings → Setup checklist** in the app and work from top to bottom. Each step has a button that opens the right screen, and a tick appears when it's done. It takes about 5 minutes.

## 1. Getting started

When you first open Clarity Recovery, it asks a few short questions:

| Step | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The app works in 14 languages: English, हिन्दी, मराठी, Español, العربية, Português, 中文, Français, বাংলা, Русский, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, Deutsch and 日本語. |
| **About you** | Name or nickname (optional), gender and age range, to tailor the app. |
| **Country** | Shows crisis helplines and support websites for where you live. |
| **Self-check** | 8 private questions about your habits. Your answers set up a starting plan: reminders, protection and a goal. Clarity is not a diagnosis tool. |
| **Your reasons** | Why you want to quit. Clarity shows these to you in tough moments. |
| **Faith (optional)** | Turn on Bible verses and prayer if you'd like them. |

You can change all of this later in **Settings**.

## 2. The setup checklist

**Where:** Settings → **Setup checklist**, the **Finish setting up Clarity** card on the Today screen, or the button at the top of the Protection screen.

The checklist groups everything into four parts:

| Group | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| **Essentials** | Allow notifications · Add an accountability partner · Keep SOS one tap away |
| **Protection** | Website blocker · Always-on VPN (optional) · Mindful pause |
| **Keep Clarity running** (Android) | Battery optimization · Autostart, background pop-ups and locking in recent apps (only shown on phones that need them) |
| **Optional extras** | Fitness tracker · Reminders |

- **Green tick:** done. Clarity checks most steps itself and updates the tick when you come back from your phone's settings.
- **Empty circle:** still to do. Tap the button on the card.
- **"I've done this"** appears on steps Android doesn't let apps check, such as Autostart on Xiaomi phones. Tap it once you've done the step.

## 3. Protection: website blocker

The blocker stops adult websites from loading in **every browser and app** on your phone.

### Android (one tap)

1. Open **Protection** (from Settings, or the protection card on Today).
2. Tap **Turn on blocker**, read the short note, and tap **Continue**.
3. Android asks to allow a **VPN connection**. Tap **OK**.
4. A small **key icon** appears in your status bar. That means the blocker is on.

**How it works:** the "VPN" only sends website-name lookups (DNS) to **CleanBrowsing Family Filter**, which refuses adult sites. Your browsing itself does **not** go through it, and Clarity doesn't record which sites you visit.

**Keep it on after a restart (recommended):**
1. In Protection, tap **Keep it on after restarts** (or Setup checklist → *Keep the blocker on after a restart*).
2. Tap the **gear ⚙** next to *Clarity Recovery*.
3. Turn on **Always-on VPN**.

> Only one VPN can run at a time on Android. If you use another VPN app, turning it on switches the blocker off. In that case you can use Android's **Private DNS** setting instead: Settings → Network & internet → Private DNS → *Private DNS provider hostname* → `family-filter-dns.cleanbrowsing.org`. The Protection screen has a **Copy address** button for this.

### iPhone

1. Open **Protection** and tap **Install blocker profile**, then allow the download.
2. Open the **Settings** app and tap **Profile Downloaded** near the top.
3. Tap **Install** and enter your passcode.
4. Adult sites are now blocked in Safari and other apps.

## 4. Protection: mindful pause for social apps

*(Android, part of Clarity Pro.)* When you open an app you've chosen, such as Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, Snapchat, Facebook, Telegram, YouTube or Discord, Clarity first shows a calm full-screen pause. It has a breathing circle and your reasons, plus three choices:

- **Leave [app]** takes you to your home screen.
- **I'm having an urge** opens the SOS screen.
- **I still want to open it**, then **Open [app] for 10 minutes**. The two taps are on purpose: they give you a moment to choose.

The back button counts as *Leave*.

### Turn it on

1. Open **Protection** → **Mindful pause** and choose the apps to pause.
2. Tap **Turn on mindful pause**, read the note, and tap **Open settings**.
3. In Accessibility, tap **Installed apps** (or **Downloaded apps**).
4. Tap **Clarity Recovery – Mindful pause** and switch it **on**. Confirm when Android asks.
5. Come back to Clarity. The Protection screen now shows the pause as on.

**Privacy:** the accessibility permission is used only to notice **which app** is opened. Clarity never reads your messages, what you type or what's on screen, and nothing leaves your phone.

### "Restricted setting" or a greyed-out switch (Android 13 and newer)

If you installed Clarity from an APK file rather than the Play Store, Android blocks this switch at first. To allow it:

1. Open **Protection** and tap **Open App info** (or go to Settings → Apps → Clarity Recovery).
2. Tap **⋮** (top right) → **Allow restricted settings**, and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
3. Go back to Accessibility and switch on **Clarity Recovery – Mindful pause**.

### iPhone

iPhone doesn't let apps pause other apps. Use Screen Time instead: **Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit → Social**, and set a short daily limit such as 15 minutes.

## 5. Keep Clarity running on your phone brand

Some phone brands close apps in the background to save battery. That can switch off the blocker or the mindful pause without telling you. Clarity detects these phones and adds the steps below to your **Setup checklist** and **Protection** screen, with buttons that open the right setting.

### Everyone on Android

- **Battery optimization:** Setup checklist → *Stop battery saving from closing Clarity* → set Clarity Recovery to **Don't optimize** (or **Unrestricted**).

### Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)

1. **Autostart:** Setup checklist → **Open Autostart** → switch on *Clarity Recovery*. Or: Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Clarity Recovery → Autostart.
2. **Background pop-ups:** **Open permissions** → **Other permissions** → allow **Display pop-up windows while running in background**.
3. **Battery saver:** App info → **Battery saver** → **No restrictions**.
4. **Lock in recent apps:** open recent apps, pull Clarity's card **down** (or long-press it) and tap the **lock** 🔒.

### Samsung (One UI)

- Settings → Apps → Clarity Recovery → **Battery** → **Unrestricted**.
- Settings → Battery → **Background usage limits**: make sure Clarity Recovery is **not** in *Sleeping apps* or *Deep sleeping apps*.

### OPPO, realme (ColorOS / realme UI) and OnePlus (OxygenOS)

- App info → **Battery usage** → allow **background activity** (and **auto launch** / **Allow auto-launch** where shown).
- Lock Clarity in recent apps (tap the ⋮ or long-press its card → **Lock**).

### vivo, iQOO (Funtouch OS / OriginOS)

- Settings → Battery → **Background power consumption management** → Clarity Recovery → **Allow**.
- i Manager → App manager → **Autostart manager** → allow Clarity Recovery.
- Lock Clarity in recent apps.

### Huawei, Honor

- Settings → Battery → **App launch** → Clarity Recovery → switch to **Manage manually** and allow **Auto-launch**, **Secondary launch** and **Run in background**.

> Menu names change between versions. If you can't find one, search your phone's Settings for **"autostart"**, **"battery"** or **"background"**.

**If protection stops after a while:** open Accessibility, switch **Clarity Recovery – Mindful pause** off and on again, then check the steps above.

## 6. The panic button (SOS)

**Get to it fast:**
- the round **SOS** button in the middle of the bottom bar, on every tab
- the red **I'm having an urge** card on Today
- **long-press the Clarity app icon** → **Panic button**
- an **SOS icon on your home screen**: Setup checklist → *Keep SOS one tap away* → **Add SOS to home screen** (Android)

**What you'll find:**
- A **breathing circle** to slow down, and a **10-minute timer**. Urges usually peak and fade within 10–20 minutes.
- **Your reasons**, and quick things to do right now (leave the room, cold water, push-ups…).
- **Text your partner:** opens a ready-to-send message in your messages app. Tap **Send** (Android and iPhone don't let apps send texts without you). You can also **Call** them.
- **Scripture and a short prayer**, if faith content is on.
- **The urge passed. I won** adds to your *urges beaten* count. **I slipped** opens a kind, no-shame log.
- **Crisis helplines** for your country, if you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself.

## 7. Accountability partner

**Where:** Support tab → **Accountability**.

1. Enter your partner's **name** and **phone number** (with country code, e.g. +91…).
2. Tap **Save partner**.
3. Keep **Text them when I press panic** on, so the message is ready the moment you tap SOS.
4. Use **Send weekly update** (by text) or **Share weekly update** (email, WhatsApp, Signal…) to send them your streak, urges beaten, slips and check-ins for the week.

Choose someone you trust, and tell them beforehand what the message means and how you'd like them to respond.

## 8. Daily use: streak, check-ins, journal

- **Today:** your streak (days and hours), progress to the next milestone (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days…), best streak, urges beaten and your goal.
- **Daily check-in:** mood, urge level (0–10), triggers, a short note and something you're grateful for. It takes about a minute, and once a day helps you spot patterns.
- **Journal:** your history, 7-day averages and your most common triggers.
- **Slips:** logging one resets the streak but keeps your best streak and history. Each slip asks what triggered it and your plan for next time. A slip is information, not failure.

## 9. Wellness, research and stories

- **Guided sessions:** meditation (calm breathing, urge surfing, body scan, self-compassion, grounding, gratitude), yoga flows, desk stretches, bodyweight workouts and a brisk-walk plan, all timed step by step.
- **Sound and voice:** a calm voice reads each step aloud, a bell marks each new step, and soft tones pace the breathing circle. Turn them on or off from the session screen, or choose a female or male voice and the speed in Settings → Sound & voice. The voice comes from your phone, so it speaks your app language and works offline. Bible verses and daily thoughts have a **Listen** button. On iPhone, sound follows the silent switch.
- **Diet tips** and a **daily habits** checklist (meditation, exercise, yoga, eating well, water, connection, phone-free bedroom).
- **Research & facts:** short summaries of published studies, each with a link to the original paper. New ones appear automatically.
- **Stories of freedom:** real stories from people who got free, shared with their written permission. We never invent testimonials. You can share yours from the same screen.

## 10. Reminders and notifications

**Where:** Settings → Reminders. Allow notifications when asked.

- **Encouraging reminders:** short messages every **1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 hours**, between a start time (6–10 am) and a last time (8–11 pm).
- **Daily check-in reminder** at a time you choose.
- **Free trial reminder:** 2 days before your free month ends.
- Reminder texts are **discreet**: they never mention porn, so they're safe on a lock screen.

If notifications don't arrive, see [Troubleshooting](#17-troubleshooting).

## 11. Fitness tracker and smartwatch

*(Optional, part of Clarity Pro.)* **Where:** Settings → Fitness & smartwatch.

- **Android:** uses Google's **Health Connect**. Fitbit, Samsung Health, Garmin and Wear OS can sync into it. Install or update Health Connect from the Play Store if Clarity asks.
- **iPhone:** uses **Apple Health**.
- Clarity reads **steps, sleep and heart rate** only, on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

## 12. Instagram follow check

*(Part of Clarity Pro.)* This finds accounts you follow that may post adult content, so you can unfollow them. Clarity never logs in to Instagram. It reads the file Instagram gives you, on your phone.

1. In Instagram: profile → menu ☰ → **Accounts Center**.
2. **Your information and permissions** → **Download your information**.
3. Choose **Some of your information** → **Followers and following**.
4. **Download to device**, format **JSON**, then **Create files**.
5. When Instagram emails you (usually within minutes), download the **.zip** file.
6. In Clarity, open **Instagram follow check** → **Choose file**.

Matches are based on account names only, so check each one before unfollowing. Tap an account to open it in Instagram.

## 14. Language, country, themes and colours

- **Language:** Settings → Language. The app restarts to apply it. Arabic and Urdu use a right-to-left layout.
- **Country:** Settings → Gender, age & country. It changes which helplines and support websites you see.
- **Appearance:** Settings → Appearance. Choose a theme (**Serene, Dawn, Ocean, Forest, Sand**), **Light / Dark / System**, and an accent colour. *(Themes and colours are part of Clarity Pro.)*

## 15. Free trial and subscription

- **Your first month is free.** After that, choose **Monthly**, **6 months** or **Yearly**. Prices are shown in your local currency and set for your country.
- **Always free, with no subscription:** the panic button, helplines, streak tracker, check-ins and the website blocker.
- **Clarity Pro adds:** all guided sessions, the mindful pause, the Instagram check, fitness tracker insights, themes and colours, and new research and stories.
- **Reminder:** you get a notification 2 days before the trial ends. *Settings → Subscription* shows the date.
- **Manage or cancel:** Settings → Subscription. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged (on iPhone, at least 24 hours before). You can also cancel in the Play Store (**Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**) or on iPhone (**Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**).
- **New phone?** Sign in with the same Google / Apple account. Your subscription is restored from the store.

## 16. Privacy

- **No account and no servers for your data.** Your streak, journal, check-ins and settings are stored **only on your phone**.
- The blocker sends website-name lookups to CleanBrowsing's filter. Clarity doesn't see or store the sites you visit.
- The mindful pause only notices which app is opened.
- Health data is read on your phone and never uploaded.
- **Erase everything:** Settings → **Erase all data**. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## 17. Troubleshooting

### The mindful pause doesn't appear
1. **Setup checklist:** is *Turn on the mindful pause* ticked? If not, follow its button.
2. **Is the app selected?** Protection → Mindful pause → the app's chip shows a ✓.
3. **Greyed-out switch / "Restricted setting":** see [section 4](#4-protection-mindful-pause-for-social-apps).
4. **Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, realme, OnePlus, Huawei, Honor:** do every step in [section 5](#5-keep-clarity-running-on-your-phone-brand).
5. **Accessibility says "on" but nothing happens:** your phone froze the service. Switch it **off and on** again in Accessibility.
6. **You chose "Open for 10 minutes" recently:** the pause comes back after those 10 minutes, the next time you open the app.
7. Open Clarity once after an update, so the pause gets your language and colours.

### The website blocker turns off
- Turn on **Always-on VPN** ([section 3](#3-protection-website-blocker)).
- Another VPN app replaces it. Use **Private DNS** instead.
- Do the battery steps for your phone ([section 5](#5-keep-clarity-running-on-your-phone-brand)).

### A site still loads
- Close and reopen the browser, or clear its cache. It may have saved the site earlier.
- Check the key icon 🔑 is in the status bar.
- Some browsers use their own "secure DNS". In Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → **Use secure DNS** → off (or *With your current service provider*).
- No filter is perfect. Report sites that get through, and we'll pass them on.

### No notifications
- Setup checklist → **Allow notifications**.
- Phone Settings → Apps → Clarity Recovery → Notifications → allowed.
- Do the battery steps in [section 5](#5-keep-clarity-running-on-your-phone-brand). Some phones delay or block notifications from apps they've put to sleep.

### The partner text didn't send
Clarity opens your messages app with the text ready. **You need to tap Send.** Android and iPhone don't let apps send texts without you.

### The SOS home-screen icon wasn't added
Some launchers don't support adding shortcuts. Long-press the Clarity icon instead, then drag **Panic button** onto your home screen.

### The subscription isn't recognised
Open Settings → Subscription and tap **Restore purchases** (on the subscription screen, or inside *Manage* if you already see an active plan). Use the same Google / Apple account you subscribed with.

## 18. FAQ

**Is Clarity a replacement for therapy?**
No. It's a daily companion. If you're struggling, please consider a therapist or support group. The Support tab lists helplines for your country.

**Can someone see that I use this app?**
Notifications are discreet, and nothing is shared unless you choose to text your partner.

**Does the blocker slow my internet?**
No. Only website-name lookups go to the filter; your browsing goes directly.

**Can I turn protection off?**
Yes, any time in Protection. Clarity is there to support your choices, not to lock you in.

**I slipped. Do I lose everything?**
No. Your best streak and history stay. Log it honestly, look at what triggered it, and start again. Every day counts.
